Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Rocco's Academy ("the app") helps you train your dog. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

Data stored on your device

Your dogs' profiles, training progress, plan, journal, and settings are saved on your device. You can delete them anytime with "Reset all data" in your profile.

Optional account and cloud backup

Accounts are optional. If you sign in with Apple or with an emailed one-time code, we store your account ID (a private Apple identifier or a hashed email), your email address and name if provided, and a copy of your app data (dogs, progress, plan, journal, streak, and health records you entered) on Cloudflare servers so you can back it up and restore it on another device. Sign-in codes are stored only as a hash and expire after 10 minutes. You can delete your account and all cloud backup data anytime with "Delete account" on the Account screen; deletion is immediate and does not remove data stored on your device or cancel subscriptions.

Health and medical records

Health information you enter for your dogs (conditions, medications, allergies, weight, vet details and dose logs) is stored only on your device, or in your own account backup if you create one. We don't share or sell it. It's only sent to the Ask Rocco coach if you turn on "Include active conditions & meds", and only to answer that question. A medical summary is shared only when you choose to share or print it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to check your subscription status. We never see or store your payment details.

Suggestions

If you send a suggestion, we store its text, category, rating, and (if you add it) your email so we can follow up.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, and we don't use advertising trackers. Some gear links may be affiliate links to Amazon, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

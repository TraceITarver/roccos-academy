Rocco's Academy Support

Need help with the app, a subscription, or have a suggestion? Email us at roccosacedemymysupport@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Subscriptions & refunds

Rocco's Academy Plus is billed by Apple. Manage or cancel anytime in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the Plus screen and tap "Restore purchases". Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your training progress and dog records are saved on your device. You can erase them anytime in the Dog tab → "Reset all data". See our Privacy Policy.

Ask Rocco

Ask Rocco gives general, AI-generated training tips. It is not a veterinarian. For health concerns, always talk to your vet.
